Frequently Asked Questions

What are AI search optimization services?

AI search optimization services improve how your brand appears everywhere people search, from Google and Bing results to AI answer engines and chatbots. At Techno Exponent, this approach, also called Search Everywhere Optimization, combines four disciplines: SEO, AEO, GEO and AIO, managed as one strategy.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO, GEO and AIO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) improves rankings in traditional search results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) structures content so it is selected as the direct answer in featured snippets, voice assistants and People Also Ask. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) helps your brand get mentioned and cited by generative AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot. AIO (AI Optimization) focuses on visibility inside AI-powered search features such as Google AI Overviews and AI Mode. Together they cover every place your customers search.

Is SEO still relevant now that people use AI search?

Yes. AI search tools and Google AI Overviews rely heavily on pages that are crawlable, well-structured and already trusted by search engines. Strong technical SEO, quality content and authority remain the foundation that AEO, GEO and AIO build on.

How do you get a brand cited in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity?

We make your content easy for AI systems to find, understand and trust: clear answer-first writing, structured data, strong entity signals, original data, consistent brand information across the web and mentions on authoritative third-party sites. We then track how often and how accurately AI tools reference your brand.

How long does it take to see results from AI search optimization?

Technical fixes and answer-focused content updates can show early movement within a few weeks. Sustained ranking growth and consistent AI citations typically build over three to six months, depending on competition, site health and content depth.

How do you measure AI search visibility?

Alongside rankings, organic traffic and conversions, we track brand mentions and citations across AI platforms for a defined set of prompts, share of voice versus competitors, featured snippet and AI Overview presence, and referral traffic from AI tools in GA4.

Do you optimize for Google AI Overviews and AI Mode?

Yes. Our AIO service focuses on the signals that help pages appear as sources in Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, including topical depth, concise answers to follow-up questions, structured data, page experience and demonstrated E-E-A-T.

Can you combine these services with PPC, content and social media marketing?

Yes. As part of our integrated digital marketing services, search and AI visibility work is aligned with paid media, content marketing, social media and reputation management so every channel supports the same growth goals.

Share your website and goals through the contact form or schedule a meeting. We start with a free discussion, then run a search and AI visibility audit and recommend a plan that fits your business.
