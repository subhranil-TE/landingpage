Why Do Shares & Dividends Get Transferred to IEPF?

The Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF) receives shares and dividends from companies under the Companies Act, 2013, for several common reasons — often without the investor ever realizing it.

Unclaimed Dividends for 7+ Years

Automatic statutory transfer to the MCA once dividends remain unclaimed for seven consecutive years.

Change of Address

Unreceived company communications and dividend warrants after an investor relocates.

Death of Original Shareholder

Succession and legal heir complexities that leave holdings unclaimed by the family.

Dormant / Inoperative Bank Accounts

Bounced electronic dividend credits when the linked bank account is no longer active.

Name / Signature Mismatch

Rejection by Registrars (RTA) when records do not match current KYC documents.

Physical Certificates Lost / Damaged

Inability to dematerialize shares when original physical certificates cannot be located.
